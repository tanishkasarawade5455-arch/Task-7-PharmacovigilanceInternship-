MAIN BODY SUMMARY

1. PURPOSE
This document provides an audit-ready summary of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) based on the supplied organisational checklist, system inventory and SOP register.

2. ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE AND QPPV OVERSIGHT
The pharmacovigilance function is governed through collaboration between the Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance (QPPV), Safety Operations, Quality Assurance and Information Technology functions.

QPPV Responsibilities:
• Oversight of pharmacovigilance compliance.
• Case intake and medical review governance.
• Aggregate reporting oversight.
• Vendor oversight and compliance monitoring.
• Audit, CAPA and inspection readiness activities.
• Business continuity oversight.
• Escalation and management review of safety issues.

Supporting Functional Areas:
• Safety Operations: signal management, literature monitoring and case processing.
• Quality Assurance: quality system administration, audits and CAPA management.
• Information Technology: validated system support, access management and backup controls.

3. SOURCES OF SAFETY DATA
Safety information may originate from spontaneous reports, literature monitoring, regulatory authorities, partners, vendors, clinical programmes and post-marketing surveillance activities.
